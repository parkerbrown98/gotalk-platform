## 1. What the site is

The site explains Gotalk, offers downloads, and provides a public index: a copy of public forum content from
Gotalk communities that opted in. We do not host those communities. Each is run by its own operator on its own
server, under its own terms.

- Software downloads are governed by their open-source licenses (server: GNU AGPL-3.0, client: MIT), not by

## 3. Communities and operators who list

If you operate a Gotalk server and register it:

- You confirm you control the origin and are allowed to list its public content.
- You are responsible for the content on your server and for having the rights and notices you need from your
  members, including a notice that public places may appear in search engines.
- You can stop listing at any time by turning off Public directory in your instance settings. Pages are removed
  within an hour in normal operation and 24 hours at most.
- We can refuse, suspend or remove a listing at any time, for example for spam, illegal content, abuse of the
  index or a failed quality check. We don't owe a reason for the quality check, but we will give one for a
  suspension where we can.
